Printing at true size. PAN, Aadhaar PVC, Voter ID (EPIC), driving licence and debit cards all share the ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 format — 85.60 × 53.98 mm. The PDF places each card at those exact millimetres, so in the print dialog choose Actual size or Scale 100% and switch off “Fit to page” — otherwise the printer will shrink the sheet by a few percent and the card comes out undersized. Cards render at 600 dpi. For a laminated card, print front and back alternating, cut on the marks and stick the pair back to back.
